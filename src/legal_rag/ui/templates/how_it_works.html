A simple way to research the record

How this service works

Use this service to explore public M&A court opinions and SEC agreement filings. It helps you find relevant source material, then lets you open the original documents yourself.

1

Ask one focused question

Name a company, agreement, case, court, or legal issue. For example: “What conditions must be met before the Microsoft and Activision merger can close?”

2

Read the response

Use the response as a starting point. It is limited to the public source material shown with it.

3

Check the sources

Open the cited court opinion or SEC filing section. Read the original wording before relying on it.

What the scores mean

Source match confirms whether the cited links shown with a response resolve to the public record. Source relevance helps sort excerpts for the question you asked. Neither score is a legal-correctness grade or legal advice.
