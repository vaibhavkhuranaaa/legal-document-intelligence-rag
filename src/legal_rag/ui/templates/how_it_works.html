Research method

Question, evidence, record.

The workspace helps you locate relevant public source material and then inspect the original wording yourself.

REVIEW SEQUENCE

A three-stage research pass

  1. 01

    Frame one focused question

    Name a company, agreement, case, court, or legal issue. A narrow brief produces a more reviewable source set.

    Input
  2. 02

    Read the bounded response

    Treat the response as a starting point limited to the public source material displayed with it.

    Analysis
  3. 03

    Check the public record

    Open the cited court opinion or SEC filing section and read the original wording before relying on it.

    Review
SCORE INTERPRETATION

Two signals, narrow meanings

Source match
Confirms whether citations shown with a response resolve to the registered public record.
Source relevance
Orders retrieved passages for the question within one result set.

Neither signal is a legal-correctness grade or legal advice.
